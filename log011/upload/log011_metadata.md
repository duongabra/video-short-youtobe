## Mô tả (EN)
Summer 1941. Germany invaded the Soviet Union, and the Red Army sent a strange weapon to the front: dogs.

For years, Soviet trainers had kept dogs hungry and hidden their food under tanks, so a tank meant dinner. In war, each dog carried 10–12 kg of explosives with a wooden lever on its back. Under a tank, the lever would trigger the charge.

But the dogs had trained beside still, silent Soviet tanks. At the front, engines roared and guns fired. According to Soviet reports, some dogs went looking for the familiar Soviet tanks, and of the first 30 dogs sent to the front, only 4 reached a German tank. Others fled back to their own trenches, still carrying their bombs.

Moscow later claimed the dogs had hit around 300 German tanks. Most historians believe the real number was far smaller.
